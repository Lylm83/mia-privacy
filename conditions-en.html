Subscription terms — MiA

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Android application “MiA”

In short: MiA is paid for by a monthly subscription, renewed automatically until cancelled, and — if you wish — by one-off credit top-ups. Everything is bought and cancelled on Google Play. Purchased credit can only be used with an active subscription; it has no expiry date while the subscription runs, and it is kept for six months after the subscription ends, then lost.

1. Purpose

These terms apply to the subscription and the top-ups of the Android application MiA, published by Lylm83 (independent developer, established in France). Contact: Lylm83@laposte.net.

By subscribing or buying a top-up, you accept these terms. Purchases are made and paid for through Google Play, whose terms of service also apply.

2. The service

MiA is a voice assistant with characters: conversation, translation, language lesson, audio guide. It requires an Android phone, an Internet connection and a Google account. It is reserved for adults.

Answers are generated by artificial intelligence: they may be inaccurate or incomplete. They do not replace professional advice, in particular on health, legal or financial matters.

3. The subscription

The subscription is monthly. Its price, all taxes included, is the one displayed by Google Play at the time of purchase. Each month of subscription includes:

  • the answers of the artificial intelligence, within a monthly usage allowance;
  • your phone's standard voice, without limit;
  • a small allowance of premium voice, to discover it.

Allowances are renewed at each billing date; what has not been used is not carried over. When the artificial-intelligence allowance is used up, the service stops until the next billing date, unless you top up; when the premium-voice allowance is used up, answers continue with the standard voice. A daily usage limit protects the service against abuse.

4. Top-ups

A top-up is a one-off purchase that adds credit, either for artificial intelligence or for premium voice. The credit obtained is displayed before purchase; it is used after the allowance included in the subscription. The usage estimates displayed are orders of magnitude.

  • credit can only be used with an active subscription;
  • it has no expiry date while the subscription runs;
  • if the subscription ends, the credit is kept for six months: it becomes usable again if you resubscribe within that time; after six months it is permanently lost, without refund;
  • credit is not refundable, not transferable to another account, and cannot be converted into money.

5. Payment and renewal

Payment is processed by Google Play, with the payment method of your Google account. The subscription is renewed automatically every month, at the current price, until it is cancelled.

If the subscription price changes, you are informed in advance by Google Play, which asks for your consent where the law requires it; you can cancel before the new price applies.

6. Cancellation

You can cancel at any time, free of charge and without giving a reason: in the application, Top up screen → “Manage my subscription”, or in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. Access remains open until the end of the period already paid for, which is not refunded pro rata.

Uninstalling the application does not cancel the subscription. Deleting your account from the application (Profile → “Sign out / Delete account”) cancels the current Google Play subscription and permanently erases any remaining credit.

A top-up is digital content supplied immediately: by buying it, you request its immediate performance and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal as soon as the credit is made available to you.

To exercise your right of withdrawal or request a refund: the Google Play refund page, or a message to Lylm83@laposte.net stating the Google Play order number (GPA…). A refunded purchase leads to the removal of the corresponding credit or access.
