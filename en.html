Privacy Policy — MiA

Last updated: 31 May 2026 · Android application “MiA”

In short: MiA runs without any server we operate. Your conversations, settings and API keys stay on your device. When you use artificial intelligence, text-to-speech or speech recognition, your data is sent directly from your phone to the third-party services you have chosen, using your own API keys. The publisher of MiA does not receive, store or sell any of this data. The app contains no analytics and no tracking.

1. Who is responsible for processing

The MiA application is published by Michael Stehly (independent developer). For any question regarding this policy or your data, you may write to: Lylm83@laposte.net.

MiA is a “client” application: it has no server operated by the publisher. For the processing carried out by third-party services (AI models, text-to-speech), you act as the user of your own accounts with those providers, through your personal API keys.

2. Principle: no collection by the publisher

  • MiA sends no data to the publisher and hosts no user account on our servers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party advertising tracking is built in, apart from what is described in the “Advertising” section below.
  • Your data is stored locally on the device and is removed together with the app when you uninstall it.

4. Android permissions

  • Internet / network state — communicate with the AI and voice services, and detect connection loss.
  • Microphone (audio recording) — only when you start voice dictation.
  • Approximate location — only if you enable the geographic context option; can be refused at any time.
  • Camera — handled through the system camera app, for the Audio guide mode.
  • Keep screen awake (wake lock / proximity sensor) — turn off the touch screen when you hold the phone to your ear during voice playback.

5. Third-party recipients

Depending on the features you use and the provider you choose, your data may be transmitted directly from your device to the following services, governed by their own privacy policies:

  • AI model providers (your choice): Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), OpenAI, Mistral AI, xAI (Grok), OpenRouter.
  • Text-to-speech: ElevenLabs.
  • Speech recognition and fonts: Google services present on the device (Android / Google Play Services).

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of each provider you enable. MiA merely forwards your requests to those services on your behalf.

6. Advertising

If enabled in this version The application may display a banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. For this purpose, AdMob may process an advertising identifier and limited technical data in order to serve ads. Processing carried out by Google is governed by Google's privacy policy.

You can permanently remove advertising through a one-time “no ads” purchase offered inside the app. Advertising is also hidden during voice exchanges so as not to disturb hands-free use.

7. Retention and deletion

All app data (conversations, settings, API keys, imported photos) is kept locally on your device for as long as you wish. You can:

  • delete a conversation or an item directly in the app;
  • clear the app data from the Android settings;
  • uninstall the app, which removes all local data.

Data already transmitted to a third-party provider is governed by that provider's retention policy; exercise your rights directly with them.

8. Security

Network exchanges with third-party services use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your API keys and data remain confined to the app's private storage on the device. As with any local storage, their protection also depends on your device's lock and security.

9. Children

MiA is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If the app is used by a minor, this must be done under the responsibility and supervision of a parent or guardian, in particular because the answers are generated by third-party artificial intelligence.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

Since the publisher neither collects nor stores your data, you retain direct control of your information: you can view, edit and erase it at any time from the app or by uninstalling it. For data processed by third-party providers, send your access, rectification or erasure requests directly to each of them. For any question, contact Lylm83@laposte.net. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

11. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or to regulations. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page. Significant changes will be indicated in the app or on the store listing.
