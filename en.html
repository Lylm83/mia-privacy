Privacy Policy — MiA

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · Android application “MiA”

In short: MiA is a subscription voice assistant. You sign in with your Google account; your requests go through the publisher's server, which forwards them to artificial-intelligence and text-to-speech services in order to answer you, and which meters your usage. The server does not keep the content of your conversations: your history stays on your phone. Your personal profile is only sent to the AI section by section, if you switched that section on; health information is only sent with your explicit consent. MiA contains no advertising and no tracking tools, and your data is neither sold nor rented.

1. Who is responsible for processing

The MiA application is published by Lylm83 (independent developer, established in France), the data controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For any question about this policy or your data: Lylm83@laposte.net.

2. How MiA works

MiA lets you talk with characters: conversation roles, translation, language lesson, audio guide. To do so:

  • you sign in with your Google account (required);
  • your requests are sent, over an encrypted connection, to the publisher's server (Google Firebase / Google Cloud, region europe-west1, Belgium);
  • this server checks your subscription, forwards the request to the artificial-intelligence or text-to-speech service, returns the answer to you, and records what the request cost;
  • the history of your conversations, your lessons, translations, visits and your profile are stored on your phone.

4. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: reaching the server, knowing whether you are offline;
  • Microphone: listening to you when you press the microphone button;
  • Approximate location: optional, see above;
  • Keeping the screen on: while something is being read aloud.

Audio-guide photos are taken with your phone's camera app or picked by you from your gallery: MiA has no permanent access to your photos.

5. Recipients and processors

Your data is only shared with the providers needed to run the service:

  • Google: hosting of the server and database (Firebase, Google Cloud, European Union), Google account sign-in, Google Play payments, Google Cloud text-to-speech, the phone's speech recognition — Google's policy;
  • OpenRouter (United States): routing your requests to the artificial-intelligence model of the chosen role (for example Mistral AI, France) and to web search — OpenRouter's policy;
  • ElevenLabs (United States) and Microsoft Azure: text-to-speech for some voices — ElevenLabs, Microsoft.

These providers receive the text to be processed, not your identity: the publisher sends them neither your e-mail address nor your account identifier. Some are established outside the European Union, notably in the United States; the transfer then relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses, EU–US Data Privacy Framework). How they process this content is governed by their own policies.

Avoid giving an artificial intelligence information you would not want these services to process (passwords, banking details, information about other people).

6. No advertising, no tracking, no resale

MiA displays no advertising, includes no audience-measurement or ad-tracking tools, and the publisher does not sell, rent or trade your data.

7. Retention periods

  • On your phone (history, profile, settings): until you delete them in the application, or uninstall it;
  • Account, subscription and credits: for the duration of your subscription; purchased credits remain usable for six months after the subscription ends, then are lost;
  • Usage and purchases: for the duration of the relationship, then for the time required by accounting and tax obligations;
  • Technical logs of the host: a few weeks.

8. Deleting your account and your data

You may delete your MiA account, and the data attached to it on the publisher's server, at any time directly in the application: Profile → very bottom → “Sign out / Delete account” → “Delete my account”. Deletion is immediate and final. If you can no longer access the application, write to Lylm83@laposte.net from the e-mail address of your Google account, with the subject “Deletion of my MiA account”: it is then done within 30 days.

  • Deleted: your account, your plan, your remaining credits and your usage counters;
  • May be kept for as long as the law requires: accounting records relating to your purchases;
  • Data stored on your phone is deleted by uninstalling the application (or from its screens); if a Google Play subscription is running, deleting the account in the application cancels it (it no longer renews; the period already paid for is not refunded); for a request by e-mail, cancel it yourself in Google Play.

9. Security

Exchanges are encrypted (HTTPS). The access keys for the AI and voice services are kept on the server, never in the application. Access to the server requires a signed-in account and an integrity attestation of the application; each subscriber can only read their own data. As no system is infallible, the publisher will inform you, and the competent authority, of any data breach posing a risk to you.

10. Minors

MiA is intended for adults (18 and over). The publisher does not knowingly collect data about minors; if you believe a minor is using the service, write to Lylm83@laposte.net.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time (in the application: the profile, health and location switches). To exercise them: Lylm83@laposte.net. You may also lodge a complaint with the data-protection authority of your country (in France, the CNIL).

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may change along with the application or the regulations. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page; a significant change will be announced in the application or on its store listing.
